## Algorithm version history

- `touch-trace-v1`: initial research-informed prototype recorded in the ledger above.
- `touch-trace-v2`: project usability revision prompted by direct playground feedback. Speed uses the larger of five-sample medians from raw-distance and 1€-filtered-position estimates, confidence reaches full value after two measured segments, target attack is 300 ms, and the last result is held for 1.8 seconds before a 3-second release. This change is independently designed, introduces no external source or copied code, and is logged through the revised `raw_speed`/`filtered_speed` meaning plus the new `feedback_held` CSV field.
- `touch-trace-v3`: project usability revision prompted by direct feedback that rapid alternating short touchscreen strokes did not accumulate speed evidence. Touch/pen strokes beginning within 900 ms preserve only recent bounded speed windows; geometry and filters reset, the off-surface displacement is excluded, and the choice is logged as `speed_continuity_active`. The experimental playground gains a read-only live 2D mapping grid, while Settings uses the canonical animated Flubber path as its draggable palette marker. This change is independently designed and introduces no external source or copied code.
- `touch-trace-v4`: project usability revision prompted by the requirement that participants provide occasional intentional gestures instead of continuous swiping. The default gated behavior closes a short window after 400 ms of inactivity, reduces the window to one representative speed/shape observation, contributes one calibration vote per qualified axis, applies a 0.12 dead zone and bounded signed `0.05..0.25` nudge, and holds the accumulated target until a later gate or Reset. The earlier version-3 response remains selectable as continuous behavior. Gate/mode/delta/calibration state is recorded in CSV. This change is independently designed and introduces no external source or copied code.
- `touch-trace-v5`: project usability revision prompted by clarification that the participant should steer the 2D point while drawing, continue until it reaches the desired state, and then hold that exact state. During an open gate, confidence-weighted evidence outside the existing 0.12 dead zone becomes a signed `0.04..0.4` units/second velocity integrated with frame `dt` while input is fresh. Gate close adds no residual step and performs only the single representative calibration update. CSV adds live-active, rate, and accumulated live-delta fields. This change is independently designed from direct product feedback and introduces no external source or copied code.
- `touch-trace-v6`: evidence-audit revision. Wolf et al.'s deliberate-drag and quick-swipe measurements are converted from pixels/second on their reported tablet to viewport diagonals/second and conservatively rounded to `0.15` and `0.80 D/s` cold-start anchors; their `log1p` midpoint is approximately `0.4387 D/s`. Gao et al. supports speed's arousal relevance, while Hannan et al., both Wampfler studies, and the reviews support participant/task/device caveats and continued adaptive p10/p90 calibration. The UI calls the resulting directions lower/hold/higher-arousal commands, displays D/s, and explicitly rejects diagnostic interpretation. No source code, affect classifier, or published decision threshold was copied.
- `touch-trace-v7`: project usability revision prompted by direct playground feedback that rapid alternating up/down swipes were under-classified as jagged and that short coherent bends were over-classified as round. The independent implementation treats turns from 120–180 degrees as increasing reversal evidence, compares only qualified on-surface directions of close touch/pen strokes, requires sustained moderate curvature for strong roundness, and logs `direction_reversal`. It never connects lifted-finger endpoints or counts the gap as speed. Existing angularity/curvature sources remain background rationale; no new external formula, threshold, source code, or publication claim was adopted for this revision.
- `touch-trace-v8`: project usability revision prompted by a phone playground trace that visibly contained large alternating corners but was still mapped round. The bounded equal-distance geometry horizon increases from 32 to 512 segments (`0.16D` to `2.56D`), allowing several screen-spanning legs to contribute simultaneously. The shape formula, privacy boundary, and CSV fields are unchanged. This independently designed correction uses no new external source or copied code.
