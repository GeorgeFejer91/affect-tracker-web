Network and privacy disclosure

The signal name and discovery room are public and must not contain a participant identifier. VDO.Ninja supplies third-party internet signaling, STUN, and possibly TURN even when WebRTC selects a direct local Wi-Fi path. Peers may learn each other’s IP addresses; relay routes may add latency. No camera, microphone, physiology, typed text, research record, or pointer trajectory is sent.

    Lab Streaming Layer
