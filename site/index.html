Response, calibration & display options

Touchscreens provide direct finger trajectories. Laptop touchpads expose an OS-accelerated cursor path rather than raw contacts.

Feedback behavior

In gated mode, keep swiping until the point reaches the state you want: fast/slow movement continuously raises/lowers arousal and round/jagged movement continuously moves valence right/left. When you stop, the point stays exactly where it reached. Each completed movement window still contributes only once to personal calibration.

Speed calibration: cold-start anchors are 0.15 viewport diagonals/s for deliberate slow movement, about 0.44 D/s for the midpoint/hold command, and 0.80 D/s for a quick swipe. Your completed movements progressively personalize that range. These are control commands—not claims that a speed diagnoses calmness or emotional arousal.

Repeated short touch/pen strokes beginning within 900 ms share speed evidence, but never join their shapes or count the lifted-finger jump.

The cursor remains visible over settings controls so this option can always be turned off.
