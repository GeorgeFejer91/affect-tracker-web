Response, calibration & display options

Touchscreens provide direct finger trajectories. Laptop touchpads expose an OS-accelerated cursor path rather than raw contacts.

Feedback behavior

In gated mode, keep swiping until the point reaches the state you want: fast/slow movement continuously raises/lowers arousal. Long, consistently curving paths move valence right toward round; rapid reversing or alternating up/down swipes move it left toward jagged. When you stop, the point stays exactly where it reached. Each completed movement window still contributes only once to personal calibration.

Speed calibration: cold-start anchors are 0.15 viewport diagonals/s for deliberate slow movement, about 0.44 D/s for the midpoint/hold command, and 0.80 D/s for a quick swipe. Your completed movements progressively personalize that range. These are control commands—not claims that a speed diagnoses calmness or emotional arousal.

Repeated short touch/pen strokes beginning within 900 ms share speed evidence and compare their on-surface direction, so up/down/up can register as jagged. Separate paths are never connected and the lifted-finger jump never counts as movement.

Snapshot vs stream: JSON is one frozen settings snapshot; Live FLUBBER is a connection that stays open for changing X/Y coordinates. Ordinary broadcast and scan are sender-or-receiver only. Synch with Universe is an explicit reciprocal exception where both people's full-scale X/Y inputs steer one shared Flubber; matching directions add up to the coordinate limits instead of being averaged. Invite a FLUBBER keeps your controls local and adds selected remote Flubbers alongside yours.

Public experimental connection: pressing a Broadcast or Scan action connects to a public VDO.Ninja discovery room. Your chosen signal name is visible to room listeners; peers may learn this device's IP address; third-party signaling and STUN/TURN may be used; relay paths add latency; and the free service has no availability guarantee. Static JSON contains the complete portable settings object, including desktop LSL metadata.
