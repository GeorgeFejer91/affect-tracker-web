Connect before entering immersive mode. When live, the incoming desktop X/Y coordinates own both axes and are applied directly without another smoothing delay. Quest tolerates short browser scheduling gaps, then holds the last position and warns instead of silently returning to controller input.

The discovery room is public. VDO.Ninja supplies signaling and may use STUN/TURN; peers may learn the headset's IP address and relay routes can add latency. No ECG, RR, heart rate, metric name, participant identifier, or source timestamp is received.

Flubber placement

When enabled, Flubber stays just above the selected controller and faces the headset. If grip tracking drops briefly, it holds its last tracked pose. The browser controls whether its native controller model remains visible.

When present, the completed CSV is sent once with Content-Type: text/csv. The endpoint must allow this site through CORS. The URL is not stored.
